1. Homepage
Hero section:
Title: “Travel Together. Share the Journey.”
Subtitle: “Find rides, buddies, and unique experiences across Sri Lanka. Post your trip or join one today.”
Buttons: Browse Trips and Post a Trip.
Section: How It Works with 3 steps: Sign Up → Post/Find a Trip → Travel Together.
Section: Why Join Us with 3 key benefits: Save money, Meet new friends, Explore more.
Section: House Rules with friendly bullet points:
Be respectful to others.
Meet in public places.
Don’t pay strangers in advance.
Report suspicious posts.
Share honest trip details.

Trip Title
From → To
Date & Time
Seats Available
Price
Region
Posted by (User Name + Profile Picture)

Add Filters and Search:
By Destination (From / To)
By Date
By Price Range
By Region

3. Post a Trip Page (Form)
Form fields:
Trip Title
From
To
Date & Time
Seats Available
Price
Region
Contact Info
Notes / Extra Details
Save form entries into the Trips database.
Only logged-in users can post trips.

Signup fields: Name, Email, Password.

Phone Number (required, unique ID)
Profile Picture
Short Bio (optional)

My Trips (trips I posted)
Trips I Joined
Edit Profile

USP Statement:
Our platform’s unique value is an ML-powered discovery feed that blends Sri Lanka’s real travel seasonality with community signals (Pins, Interested, Questions). It works from day one with Trending Trips and gets smarter with every user action, making it the #1 reason travelers will love our site.
1) Homepage Hero (show USP clearly)
Headline: Discover Sri Lanka with Smart Picks
Subtext: Real traveler trends + your travel style = trips you’ll love.
Trust line: Powered by CeylonX ML. Private, simple, helpful.
CTA buttons: Browse Trending · Set My Travel Style
2) Discovery Feed Tabs
Tabs: Trending (default) | Near You | Fresh Finds | For You
Tooltip for Trending: “Ranked by Pins, Interested, Questions + season.”
For You unlocks after ≥3 actions (pin/interested/join). First time → badge NEW.
3) Trip Card Redesign
Each card shows:
Title + Region chip (e.g., “Hill Country”)
2–3 badges (from ML signals):
⭐ Popular this week (N interested)
📌 Saved by travelers (N pins)
🌊 Surf season (East) / 🌞 Best weather now (South/West)
🐆 Safari good now / 🚂 Tea & Trains / 🧘 Ayurveda retreat
✨ New listing / 🛤 Pekoe Trail stage
Footer: Date/time, price/seats
Small hint: Why this pick → “Matches your Beach + Weekends preference” OR “Trending near you”.
4) Onboarding Lite (cold-start fix)
Show 3 quick questions on first visit (dismissible):
Vibe → Beach / Hills / City / Culture
When → Weekends / Festivals / Long holidays
Travel style → Solo / Friends / Family
Store as lightweight prefs; use only to reorder Trending and power “For You.”
5) Seasonal Logic (Sri Lanka–specific)
Dec–Mar: Promote South & West (Galle, Mirissa, Yala).
Jul–Aug: Promote East & North (Trinco, Jaffna, Arugam Bay).
Off-season (May–Jun, Oct–Nov): Highlight festivals, indoor/wellness retreats.
Copy examples:
🌞 “Best weather now (south coast)”
🌊 “East coast in season—calm seas & surf”
🌧 “Off-season = fewer crowds + deals”
6) Backend Scoring (reuse ML, change weights)
Keep current ML pipeline (popularity, freshness, safety, novelty, diversity, prefs).
Adjust weights:
Popularity (pins, interests, questions, recency): 35%
Seasonality: 20%
Freshness: 10%
Regional/Thematic diversity: 10%
Safety: 10%
Novelty: 5%
User preferences: 10%
Expose only badges + plain-text reasons to UI (never raw %).
